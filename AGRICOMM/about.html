From the farm straight to the buyer.

Agricomm is an online marketplace where farmers list what they grow and buyers find it, without long chains of middlemen in between.

Agriculture + Commerce Connected

Why we built Agricomm

Farmers work hard to grow good produce, but finding reliable buyers at a fair price is often the hardest part. Buyers face the opposite problem: they want fresh, trustworthy products and don't always know where to look.

Agricomm puts both sides in one place so farmers earn more from their work and buyers get quality produce.

Who Agricomm is for

Farmers

Create a listing with a description and photos of your products. Show buyers exactly what you have and set your own price.

Buyers

Browse products from farmers, search for what you need, and compare prices in one easy-to-scan marketplace.

How it works

1

Farmers list their products

Fill in the sell form with a description, price and pictures.

2

Buyers browse and search

Products appear as cards with the price, so you can compare quickly.

3

Buyer and farmer connect

Agree on the order directly and get the produce moving.

Our mission and values

Agricomm exists to make farming more rewarding and food easier to find. A farmer's hard work should be seen by the people who need it, and buyers deserve to know where their food comes from.

Fair prices

Farmers set their own prices and deal directly with buyers, so more of each sale stays with the people who grew the produce.

Trust and quality

Clear descriptions and photos help buyers know what they are getting, and help good farmers build a reputation.

Easy access

Agricomm works well on a phone, so a farmer in the village and a buyer in town can trade with the same simple tools.

Where we are going

We started with a simple marketplace for buying and selling. Our vision is to grow Agricomm into a platform that connects farmers, buyers and agricultural services across Uganda.
