From the farm straight to the buyer.

Agricomm is an online marketplace where farmers list what they grow and buyers find it, without long chains of middlemen in between.

Agriculture + Commerce Connected

Why we built Agricomm

Farmers work hard to grow good produce, but finding reliable buyers at a fair price is often the hardest part. Buyers face the opposite problem: they want fresh, trustworthy products and don't always know where to look.

Agricomm puts both sides in one place. Our mission is to make agriculture easier to trade in, so farmers earn more from their work and buyers get quality produce.

Who Agricomm is for

Farmers

Create a listing with a description and photos of your products. Show buyers exactly what you have and set your own price.

Buyers

Browse products from farmers, search for what you need, and compare prices in one easy-to-scan marketplace.

How it works

  1. Farmers list their productsFill in the sell form with a description, price and pictures.
  2. Buyers browse and searchProducts appear as cards with the price and any discounts, so you can compare quickly.
  3. Buyer and farmer connectAgree on the order directly and get the produce moving.
